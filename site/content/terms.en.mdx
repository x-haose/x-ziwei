## The nature of the results

- **A chart is a computed result.** It is cast by published rules and matches iztro's output field for field. That means the computation is consistent, not that a reading is accurate.
- **The knowledge pack is one reference view.** Schools differ, and the default pack is not the only way to read a chart.
- **Readings come from your AI model.** x-ziwei does not control and is not responsible for what the model says.

Zi Wei Dou Shu is traditional cultural content, and results are for cultural interest and reference only. They are not medical, legal, financial, psychological or other professional advice and should not be the basis for significant decisions.
